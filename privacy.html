Sensory privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Sensory is a personal safety app. When you need help, or when you are heading somewhere and want someone to keep an eye out, it tells the people you have chosen and shows them where you are. That only works if it handles your information carefully, so this page explains, in plain language, what Sensory collects, why, who it goes to, and how long it is kept.

Sensory is run by Lincore ("we", "us"). For anything about your information, including asking for a copy of it or for it to be deleted, email littletonlindy9@gmail.com.

The short version. Sensory only takes your location when you raise an alert, head out, or say you have arrived, never in the background. It shares that only with the people you chose. There are no adverts, no tracking, and we never sell your information. Alerts and heading-out records, including the locations in them, are deleted automatically after 90 days. Deleting your account deletes everything.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your name and email address, and a password, which is stored scrambled (hashed) so that nobody, us included, can read it. Your name is what your contacts see on your alerts.
  • Your location, only at these moments:
    • when you hold the panic button, and then regularly while that alert is active, so your contacts can see where you are and how you are moving;
    • once, when you tap Heading out;
    • once, when you tap I've arrived safe, but only if you choose to share it at that moment.
    Sensory does not track your location at any other time and does not use location in the background.
  • The people you add: for contacts who use Sensory, the link between your accounts. For contacts reached by text message, the name you give them and their phone number.
  • A notification address for your phone, so alerts and notices can reach it.
  • A record of each text message sent for you: who it went to, what kind of message it was, when, and whether it went out. This lets Sensory warn you if a text to one of your contacts did not send, so you can phone them instead.
  • Crash reports: if the app goes wrong, a technical report of the error and the type of phone it happened on, so it can be fixed. These reports do not include your name, email or location.

Who sees it

  • The contacts you choose see your alerts and heading-out notices, with your name and location. Contacts who use Sensory see them in the app.
  • Contacts reached by text message get texts from Sensory. During an alert, and if you are late back from heading out, they also get a link to a web page showing your location. That link only works until you mark yourself safe, and never for more than 24 hours. Anyone the link is forwarded to can see that page while it works, so Sensory makes the link long and random, so it cannot be guessed.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and there is no advertising or tracking in Sensory.

Services that handle it for us

Sensory relies on a few specialist services. Each only gets what it needs to do its job:

  • Supabase stores your account and Sensory's records. The data is held in Ireland, in the European Union.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) delivers notifications to phones. It handles your phone's notification address and the notification's words.
  • BulkSMS sends the text messages. It receives your contacts' phone numbers and the text of each message.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, held in Germany, in the European Union.
  • GitHub hosts the location web page that text-message contacts open. The secret part of each link never reaches GitHub. Like any website, it sees the visitor's internet address.

Some of this information is held outside South Africa, in the European Union, whose data protection laws give it a level of protection at least as strong as South Africa's.

How long it is kept

  • Alerts and heading-out records, including every location in them, and the record of texts sent, are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Location links stop working when you mark yourself safe, or after 24 hours at the latest.
  • Your account details and your contact list are kept while you have an account.
  • Deleting your account, which you can do yourself in the app's settings, deletes your account and everything linked to it straight away.

People you add as contacts

When you add someone by phone number, Sensory sends them one text saying who added them, so they are not surprised by a message later and can ask to be taken off. If you have been added and want to be removed, ask the person who added you, or email us and we will remove you.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, and object to how it is used. Email littletonlindy9@gmail.com. If you are not happy with how we handle your request, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Keeping it safe

Information travels between your phone and Sensory's services encrypted. Access rules mean each person can only reach their own records and the alerts sent to them. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to what Sensory actually needs, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Age

Sensory is for people aged 18 and over.

Sensory is not an emergency service

Sensory alerts the people you have chosen. It does not contact the police, ambulance or fire services. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Sensory handles your information, we will update this page and the date at the top. For a significant change, we will also tell you in the app.
